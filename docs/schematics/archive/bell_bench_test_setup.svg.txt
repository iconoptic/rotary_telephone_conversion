  <text x="32" y="722" class="lbl-b" fill="#1e5c1e">Step order (bell stays disconnected the whole time)</text>
  <text x="32" y="744" class="lbl-sm" fill="#1e5c1e">1. POWER OFF. DMM: ID all 4 windings first (lead colors unverified), then R(5-8) &#8776; 2x R(5-6) proves tap jumper 6-7; jumper 2-3 fitted + insulated; Q2/Q3 not D-S shorted (diode mode).</text>
  <text x="32" y="762" class="lbl-sm" fill="#1e5c1e">2. Confirm bell RED/BLACK are physically unplugged (red gap tick marks above, TP-B open). This protects the bell coil and clapper mechanism during first power-up.</text>
  <text x="32" y="780" class="lbl-sm" fill="#1e5c1e">3. Power the Pico over USB. Connect with mpremote; import bell_ir_test (it must already have SUPPLY_V = 5 -- do not run the 12V-era copy against a 5V tap, or vice versa).</text>
  <text x="32" y="798" class="lbl-sm" fill="#1e5c1e">4. Call hold(15) (steady 25Hz drive, auto-stops) so the driver runs continuously instead of a 2s burst -- easier to catch a stable reading on a DMM, which is slow to settle.</text>
  <text x="32" y="816" class="lbl-sm" fill="#1e5c1e">5. TP-B, DMM in AC VOLTS (auto-range): expect roughly 96Vac across RED vs BLACK (160G24 unloaded, 19:1). ~0V here = jumper 2-3 is series-OPPOSING: power off, swap one end.</text>
  <text x="32" y="834" class="lbl-sm" fill="#1e5c1e">6. TP-A / TP-C, scope only (a DMM cannot see this): probe a drain vs GND. Expect ~0V while on, a ~10V flat top while off, and a brief spike riding on that flat top.</text>
  <text x="32" y="852" class="lbl-sm" fill="#1e5c1e">   PASS = spike stays clearly under 60V (the STP55NF06L's rated V_DSS). If it's flirting with 60V, STOP and re-check the snubber wiring/values before going further.</text>
  <text x="32" y="870" class="lbl-sm" fill="#a03a3a">NO SCOPE ON HAND? A DMM cannot catch a microsecond-scale spike -- it only reads the (much lower) average/RMS. You are then trusting the calculated margin</text>
  <text x="32" y="888" class="lbl-sm" fill="#a03a3a">(snubber + STP55NF06L 100%-tested avalanche rating, see docs/revK_design_review/03_bell_failure_modes.md), not a direct measurement. Two cheap hedges without a scope:</text>
  <text x="32" y="906" class="lbl-sm" fill="#a03a3a">  a) run step 4 for only a few seconds at first, feeling Q2/Q3 for warmth right after (excess heat = a clue) before running it continuously;</text>
  <text x="32" y="924" class="lbl-sm" fill="#a03a3a">  b) borrow a scope for just this one check -- it is the single measurement in this whole design that a DMM structurally cannot make.</text>
  <text x="32" y="942" class="lbl-sm" fill="#1e5c1e">7. Ctrl-C stops early (bursts also auto-stop). Call vsys() while ringing (step 4 again) -- confirm the 5V rail near T1's tap doesn't sag below ~4.5V; if it does, self-powered hub.</text>
  <text x="32" y="960" class="lbl-sm" fill="#1e5c1e">8. Only once 5/6/7 look right: power off, reconnect bell RED/BLACK, power on, ring() for one real ring, listen/feel the clapper. 9. Insulate ALL HV wiring -- ~96V is a REAL shock.</text>
